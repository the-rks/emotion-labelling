Emotion Labeling Task

EECS 594 — Human Data Collection Assignment

This task uses tweets from the dair-ai/emotion dataset. You will be shown 5 tweets, one at a time, randomly selected from the dataset.

Instructions:

  1. Read each tweet.
  2. Select the single emotion you think the person who wrote it is expressing: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  3. Base your answer on the writer's emotion, not your own reaction.
  4. There is no skip option, please choose the closest match.
  5. Tweets are shown exactly as written (typos included).

What is recorded: your participant ID, the tweets you were shown, your selected label for each one, how long you took, and the submission time.
