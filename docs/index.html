Which emotion is this person expressing?

A short annotation task for a course assignment on human data collection. It takes about two minutes.

What you'll do

  1. You'll see 5 tweets, one at a time, drawn at random from a public research dataset.
  2. For each one, choose the single emotion the writer is expressing: anger, fear, joy, love, sadness, or surprise.
  3. Judge the writer's emotion, not your own reaction to the tweet, and not the emotion of anyone mentioned in it.
  4. Go with your first instinct. There's no skip button, so pick the closest fit even when none is perfect.
  5. Tweets appear exactly as written, including typos and missing punctuation.

What gets recorded

Your participant ID, each tweet you saw, the label you gave it, how long you took, and the time you submitted. Nothing else — no IP address, no browser fingerprint, no Google sign-in. The data is used only for a class assignment.
